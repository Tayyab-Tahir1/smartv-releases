Install

A few clicks on each system. You only do this once; after that, SmarTV keeps itself up to date.

Mac

  1. Open the .dmg you downloaded and drag SmarTV into Applications.
  2. Open SmarTV. macOS says it can't check the app, because it isn't from the App Store. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to SmarTV. You only do this once.
  4. When macOS asks about the keychain, choose Always Allow. SmarTV keeps your IPTV password there, encrypted.

Says the app is damaged? Run this in Terminal, then open it again:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/SmarTV.app

Ubuntu

  1. Open a terminal in your Downloads folder and install the package:
    sudo apt install ./SmarTV-*-linux-amd64.deb
  2. Open SmarTV from your app menu.
  3. New versions show a banner in the app with a link to the new package.

Using the AppImage instead? Make it runnable, then start it. On Ubuntu 24.04 you may need libfuse2t64 first.

chmod +x SmarTV-*.AppImage && ./SmarTV-*.AppImage

First launch: SmarTV asks for your provider's server address, your username and your password. You can add more accounts, and optional TMDB and OpenSubtitles keys for cast details and English subtitles, in Settings.

What you get

Built for watching from the sofa as much as from the desk.

Browse the way you think
Shelves for Hollywood, Bollywood, Punjabi, Lollywood, South Indian and more, plus platform, genre, decade, quality and language filters.
Playback that holds up
Made for providers that allow one stream at a time. Drops resume where they stopped, and errors say what happened.
Fast-forward that speeds up
Tap to jump 10 seconds. Keep pressing and it scans faster, sized to the length of what you're watching.
English subtitles
Built-in tracks plus subtitles from OpenSubtitles, kept in sync when you skip around.
Your TV remote
Point the TV Remote Bridge on a Samsung TV at your computer and drive SmarTV from the sofa. It remembers your TV.
Private by design
Your accounts and keys stay on your computer, encrypted. There is no SmarTV server and no sign-up.
